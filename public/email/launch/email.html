Introducing the Starport Dream Factory. Your kids press picture buttons, and the factory turns their ideas into real, playable Starport cards: creatures, equipment and pets, each with its own one-of-a-kind picture.

How it works

1 Pick. Press picture buttons for colors, sizes, personalities and powers. Every button reads itself aloud, so even pre-readers can build on their own.
2 Watch. The factory writes fair, table-ready rules and draws the picture. Snap a photo of your kid's drawing and it brings that to life too.
3 Play. Print tarot-size cards, or six to a page, and bring them to your next Starport adventure.
